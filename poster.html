THE WORLD IN ONE DAY
Six stories that defined the day — from every corner of the globe
THURSDAY 19 NOVEMBER 2026
★ Lead story — Culture & Games

Grand Theft Auto VI launches worldwide after a 13-year wait

Rockstar’s epic returns to Vice City, unlocking at midnight in each time zone from New Zealand. Some 16 million copies were pre-ordered — about $1.5 billion in pre-sales — and critics score it 99 on Metacritic.

Law — The Hague

World court delivers Rohingya genocide verdict

The International Court of Justice rules today on whether Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017 crackdown, which drove 740,000 people to Bangladesh.

Diplomacy — China

Trump and Putin meet face to face in China

The US and Russian presidents hold one-on-one talks in Shenzhen at the APEC summit — their first meeting since Alaska in 2025 — with China’s Xi Jinping also in the room.

Science — Space

Voyager 1 is one light-day from Earth

NASA confirms its 1977 probe is the first human-made object a full light-day away — 16.1 billion miles out. A radio signal now takes an entire day to reach it.

Weather — India

Cyclone Akvan bears down on India’s east coast

Severe Cyclone Akvan crawls across the Bay of Bengal toward Andhra Pradesh and Odisha. Seven districts are on red alert for extremely heavy rain ahead of Friday’s landfall.

Society — Religion

Pew: Islam to pass Christianity as the world’s largest faith by 2035

Pew Research Center projects 2.57 billion Muslims against 2.52 billion Christians by 2035, driven mainly by higher fertility — 3.1 children per woman against 2.5.
