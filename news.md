## 1. LEAD — Grand Theft Auto VI launches worldwide (Culture / Tech)
- Headline: "Vice City opens: Grand Theft Auto VI launches worldwide"
- Fact: After a 13-year wait, Rockstar's GTA VI unlocks at midnight local
  time on 19 Nov on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, rolling across time zones
  from New Zealand; ~16m pre-ordered copies, ~$1.5bn in pre-sales
  (Sensor Tower via Reuters); 99 on Metacritic.
- Source: AP ("'Grand Theft Auto VI' finally arrives", 18 Nov) and Reuters
  ("Grand Theft Auto VI Begins Global Rollout", 19 Nov).

## 2. World court rules on Myanmar genocide case (Law / World)
- Headline: "World court delivers Rohingya genocide verdict"
- Fact: The International Court of Justice in The Hague delivers its
  judgment on 19 Nov in The Gambia v. Myanmar — whether Myanmar committed
  genocide against the Rohingya in its 2017 crackdown that drove 740,000+
  people to Bangladesh. Case filed 2019; the court has never before found
  a state responsible for genocide.
- Source: ICJ press release via Reuters/AP previews; The Brief, 19 Nov.

## 3. Trump and Putin meet one-on-one in China (Diplomacy)

- Fact: US President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin hold
  a one-on-one meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday on the sidelines of the
  APEC summit — their first since Alaska in August 2025 — with a
  trilateral session including China's Xi Jinping also scheduled.
- Source: The Brief, 19 Nov; SimpleLife.news daily digest, 19 Nov.

## 4. NASA confirms Voyager 1 is one light-day away (Science / Space)
- Headline: "Voyager 1 is one light-day from Earth"
- Fact: NASA confirms on 19 Nov that Voyager 1, launched in 1977, has
  become the first human-made object one light-day (~16.1bn miles /
  25.9bn km) from Earth; a radio signal now takes a full day to reach it.
  NASA hosts a live mission briefing at noon EST on 19 Nov.
- Source: NASA / Popular Science / EarthSky, 18–19 Nov.

## 5. Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast (Weather / Asia)
- Headline: "Cyclone Akvan bears down on India's east coast"
- Fact: Severe Cyclone Akvan crawls across the Bay of Bengal toward the
  Andhra Pradesh–Odisha coast; IMD puts seven districts on red alert for
  extremely heavy rain on 19–20 Nov, with landfall forecast around noon
  on 20 Nov and winds of 90–110 km/h.
- Source: The Hindu BusinessLine / IMD bulletins, 18–19 Nov.

## 6. Pew: Islam to become the world's largest religion by 2035 (Society)
- Headline: "Pew: Islam to pass Christianity as world's largest faith by 2035"
- Fact: Pew Research Center projections: 2.57bn Muslims vs 2.52bn
  Christians by 2035 (from 2.02bn vs 2.27bn in 2020), driven mainly by
  higher fertility (3.1 vs 2.5 children per woman, 2020–25).
- Source: Pew Research Center / AP, 18 Nov, widely reported in 19 Nov
  news roundups (NDTV, 19 Nov).
